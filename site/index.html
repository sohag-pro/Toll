What Toll does

R

Reel gate

Scrolls, swipes, arrow keys, and the on-screen next/previous buttons are all intercepted on Shorts, Reels, and TikTok. Video pauses while you solve.

F

Feed cap

YouTube's homepage and Facebook's news feed stop after two screens. Each unlock loads one more. Infinite scroll becomes finite.

L

Escalating lockout

Wrong answers cost more each time: 5s, 15s, 30s, 60s. Button-mashing gets punished. One right answer resets the streak.

P

Control panel

Per-platform, per-mode toggles in the toolbar popup. Keep Shorts gated but let the homepage run free. Changes apply live.

T

Time counter

A small pill in the top-right shows today's time on the site. Reappears every few minutes of active use. Daily totals in the popup.

·

Local-only

Nothing leaves your machine. No servers, no accounts, no telemetry. Two permissions: storage and the four supported hosts.

Here's how it looks

Install in about two minutes

Works in any Chromium browser — Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Opera. No account, no build tools.

  1. 1

    Download the latest ZIP

    Grab toll-vX.Y.Z.zip from the latest release. Unzip it. You should see a folder with manifest.json inside.

  2. 2

    Open your browser's extensions page

    chrome://extensions, brave://extensions, edge://extensions, or arc://extensions.

  3. 3

    Turn on Developer mode

    Toggle in the top-right of the extensions page. A row of buttons appears.

  4. 4

    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked. Pick the unzipped folder (the one with manifest.json). Toll is armed.

Questions

Does it send any data anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Toggle state and daily time counts live in chrome.storage.local. Uninstall wipes them.

Why math and not a delay timer?

A timer doesn't stop the reflex. You wait it out. Solving something forces a different mode for a few seconds. That's the whole trick.

Can I disable it on one site but not another?

Yes. The toolbar popup has per-platform toggles — even per-mode (Shorts vs. YouTube home cap, for example). Changes apply live.

Safari or Firefox?

Not yet. Chromium only for now.

A site changed and it stopped working.

Reel and feed layouts shift often. Open an issue with the URL and what broke.
